About

I am a Data Science master's student at Berlin University of Applied Sciences (BHT) with a bachelor's in Computer Science, and a research assistant at Fraunhofer IEE. I work with large language models and data engineering, building end-to-end applications with retrieval-augmented generation and agent workflows.

My research interest is computer vision, particularly for medical imaging. My thesis is on generative image translation for aerial imagery, and I want to take research like this into applications people use.
